# Foundational History - Undecided

The old excerpt discusses a **possible unifying myth or foundational catastrophe** that would have different legacies across societies. It was discussed as a direction, **not confirmed as a setting fact**.

## Why this might help
A major past event can explain religious divergence, political claims, migrations, geography, and living conflicts. Different peoples may inherit contradictory but sincerely believed accounts.

## Not established
- Whether there was a catastrophe at all
- Its causes, nature, date, witnesses, or surviving artifacts
- Whether gods, mortals, or both were responsible
