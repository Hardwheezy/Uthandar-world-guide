# Dwarven Pantheon Notes

*Research / working pantheon, not confirmed canon.*

| Deity | Domain / association |
| --- | --- |
| **Gorvan, Lord of Living Fire** | Head deity; fire, smithing, craft, kingship, sacred law; “reforges” dwarves in reincarnation |
| **Mara, Mother of Stone** | Gorvan's wife; earth, mines, fertility, burial, ancestry, marriage |
| **Avar, the Open Hand** | Taran's twin; herds, farming, wealth, hospitality, trade, peace |
| **Taran, the Closed Hand** | Avar's twin; hunting, warriors, boundaries, vengeance, protection |
