# Elven Pantheon Notes

*Research / working pantheon, not confirmed canon.*

| Deity | Domain / association |
| --- | --- |
| **Ashurak** | Head god, sun; king is his earthly vessel |
| **Kumara** | Earth mother; crops, herds, burial, regeneration |
| **Tazur** | Thunder, lightning, conquest, destruction; military aristocracy; captive sacrifice |
| **Nimara** | Moon, tides, prophecy, astronomy, dreams, lunar cycle; royal women |
| **Uram** | Water, ocean and rivers, trade, irrigation, flooding, foreign lands |
| **Ezaku** | Writing, medicine, mathematics, secret names, magic |
| **Kuthar** | Dead, tombs, darkness, hidden treasures |
