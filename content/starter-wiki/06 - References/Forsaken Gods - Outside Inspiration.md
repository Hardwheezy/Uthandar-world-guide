# Forsaken Gods - Outside Inspiration

**Not canon for this original world.** This page records a narrative premise discussed in the old chat.

In the source primer, **Tanahu** is a land where an ancient elven ruler called the **Cursing King** condemned the world in order to check the demographic decline of his conquering people. His actions affect fertility and ecology, and all surviving societies inherit the consequences. The primer includes competing religions, conquered populations, old betrayals, independent polities, ancient ruins, and gods whose names were suppressed.

## Why it was discussed
- A world defined by the long aftermath of a consequential decision.
- Morally comprehensible but monstrous rulership.
- Different cultures interpreting inherited history differently.
- A world where daily life persists under sweeping catastrophe.

**Do not copy:** Tanahu, its locations, deity names, peoples, curse mechanics, or chronology into original canon without an explicit design decision. See [[Foundational History - Undecided]].
