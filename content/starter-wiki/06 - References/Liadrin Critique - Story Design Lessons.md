# Liadrin Critique - Story Design Lessons

**External criticism of World of Warcraft storytelling; not canon setting lore.**

The old discussion treated the critique of Liadrin's death as a way to examine:
- A character's death needing acknowledgment and earned emotional sequence.
- The gap between a writer's intended theme and the audience's experienced scene.
- The difference between elite diplomatic gestures and broad societal reconciliation.
- Establishing and paying off character motivations rather than using characters as symbols.
- Continuing institutional and social consequences after dramatic events.

The old assistant explicitly distinguished these useful points from the critic's broader denunciations. See [[Storytelling Principles]].
