# Creative Brief

## Current direction (interpretation, not finalized canon)

Develop **Uthandar**, an original tabletop fantasy world with **Bronze Age / early Iron Age-inspired** material cultures and institutions. The world should be able to **generate meaningful stories through its history and the independent motivations of its peoples and factions**, rather than exist only to deliver a prewritten epic plot.

### Priorities visible in the transferred chat
- Peoples, gods, rulers, institutions, and settlements should have their own motivations.
- Politics, faith, economics, migration, and warfare should influence one another.
- Large events should result in durable consequences across society.
- Dramatic character arcs must feel causally and emotionally earned.
- Player characters may influence events, but the world does not wait for them.

### Still undecided
- Overall cosmology, maps, precise history, rules system, and whether a particular catastrophe belongs in canon. The **world is named Uthandar**, while the continent is intentionally unnamed.
- The degree to which Greg's sketches become the final world design.
- Whether inspiration from *Forsaken Gods* is adapted at all.
