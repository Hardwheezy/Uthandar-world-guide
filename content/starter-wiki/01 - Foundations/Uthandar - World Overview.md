# Uthandar

**Uthandar** is the agreed name of the fantasy **world** in which this setting takes place.

## Geographic naming decision

The principal **continent remains unnamed**. That is a deliberate choice, not an omitted placeholder that needs to be automatically filled. Instead of assuming one universally accepted continental name, inhabitants may use the names of their own kingdoms, peoples, regions, waterways, and traditional territories. The names and boundaries of those regions have **not yet been established**.

This approach is loosely inspired by historical geography, but is a **creative decision**, not a claim that ancient people never named large landmasses.

## What is not established

- No official continent name; **Estara** is an earlier suggestion, *not* adopted as the continent's name.
- No confirmed continental map, borders, kingdoms, or regional nomenclature.
- No finalized cosmology or foundational catastrophe.

## Working setting locations and people

- **Tallwater**, the **Mercer family**, and **Harlan** are examples previously raised by the author for player-versus-DM lore presentation. Detailed facts about their history, positions, and objectives must be supplied or verified before publication.
