# Open Questions

These are **unresolved**, not omissions to be filled by the assistant without consent.

- The **world is named Uthandar** (resolved). The main continent deliberately has **no single established name** (resolved naming approach). What names do particular peoples use for their own regions?
- Which elements of Greg's Bronze Age notes are now accepted canon?
- How directly do gods act in the world? Is divine kingship literal, symbolic, or contested?
- Did a world-shaping catastrophe occur? What do different peoples believe happened?
- What cultural, geographic, and economic relationships connect dwarves, humans, elves, giants, and seafaring peoples?
- Is the intended focus a single region, multiple interconnected regions, or a whole world?
- What role, if any, does a D&D-like rule system play in setting assumptions?
- Which people will edit the site? Intended audiences include general readers/players and authorized DMs; edit permissions still need design.
- What facts from the **rest** of the old conversation still need recovering?
