# Living World and Consequences

## Core idea
Build factions, settlements, cultures, rulers, religious institutions, and trade relationships with competing goals, pressures, and limits. They pursue their interests even offscreen.

## Illustrative situations from prior discussion
These are **examples, not canon historical events**:
- A city-state experiences a succession crisis.
- A temple questions royal legitimacy.
- A migrating people enters cultivated lands.
- A war leader dies and a personal coalition dissolves.
- A king's death causes disputes among heirs, priests, tributaries, and neighbors.

## Practical simulation method (suggestion)
For each major faction record:
- Goals
- Resources
- Constraints
- Leadership and succession
- Relationships and grievances
- What happens if nobody intervenes
- How player actions could alter this
