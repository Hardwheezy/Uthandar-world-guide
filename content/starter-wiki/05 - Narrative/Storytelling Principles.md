# Storytelling Principles

These design lessons were articulated in the previous conversation; they are principles, **not setting events**.

- **Epic stories emerge from a functioning world.** The setting should support many campaigns, not require one fixed storyline.
- **People act like people, not mouthpieces for themes.** Respect established loyalties, fears, obligations, and reactions.
- **Consequences propagate.** A ruler's death may trigger inheritance disputes, tax crises, political upheaval, and war.
- **Collective change is difficult.** Two leaders agreeing to peace does not erase communal trauma, land disputes, and material grievances.
- **Show rather than announce the theme.** Let lived decisions express meaning.
- **Characters have arcs** with earned decisions, losses, and consequences.
- **The world moves without the party.** Player choices still matter and can alter outcomes.
