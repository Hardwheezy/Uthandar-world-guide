# Elven Cultural Notes

These are **Greg's inspiration notes**, not independently ratified world canon.

- Mesopotamian and Moche cultural references.
- Chariots and war bows.
- Elven nobility with human subjects.
- God-kings, more precisely **divine-vessel kings** (as described in the notes).
- Elaborate headdresses.

Religion: [[Elven Pantheon Notes]].

## Development questions
What does being a divine vessel entail? How is kingship inherited or challenged? What obligations do subject communities owe temples and palaces?
