# Sea Peoples Cultural Notes

Provisional label **“Sea People”** comes from Greg's notes; not necessarily the final culture name.

- Māori, Nordic Bronze Age, and historical Sea Peoples as inspirations.
- Tattoos of stars, waves, birds, fish, and sea lions.
- Rørby swords.
- Grass and feather cloaks.
