# Dwarven Cultural Notes

These are **Greg's inspiration notes**, not independently ratified world canon.

- La Tène and Urnfield culture references.
- Cremation urns buried beneath homes.
- Iron weapons rare but available.
- **Temple-forge** as a major religious / civic institution.
- Ogham-style runes.
- Hillforts.
- Possible shared cultural complex between dwarves, humans, and giants (tentative).

Religion: [[Dwarven Pantheon Notes]].

## Development questions
How do household ancestor rites relate to the temple-forge? Who controls smithing, sacred law, and burial rights? Does a shared dwarf-human-giant cultural sphere exist?
